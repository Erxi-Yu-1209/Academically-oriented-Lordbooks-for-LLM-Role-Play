# Moral Ethics 2.1 (`道德伦理学2.1`)


[ ← Back to Repository Index ](https://www.google.com/search?q=../README.md) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [ English ](README.md) &nbsp;•&nbsp; [ 简体中文 ](README-zh.md)

**A 72-entry normative moral philosophy, symbolic interactionist psychology (`I` vs. `Me`), Gestalt therapy, and trauma-bias reasoning engine for SillyTavern roleplay.**

## 1. Overview & Core Function

* **Source Files:** `Moral Ethics 2.1.json` (English) / `道德伦理学2.1.json` (Chinese)
* **Total Entries:** **72 Entries** (`3 Constant` core gates/engines / `69 Keyword-Triggered` philosophical, psychological, linguistic, and clinical modules)
* **Injection Architecture:**
  * **Depth `@D0` (`UID 0, 16, 17, 18`):** Master moral logic gate, module selector, 4-step `<think>` ethical Chain-of-Thought template, and `<Moral_Audit>` ledger panel placed right at the bottom of the prompt for high-priority execution.
  * **Depth `@D1` (`UID 68–71`):** Gestalt therapy (`Cycle of Experience`, `Boundary Disturbances`, `Unfinished Business`) and `[SELF-REASONING]` personal experience bias & delayed trauma recognition.
  * **Depth `@D2` & `@D3` (`UID 1–15`, `UID 19–67`):** 15 chapters of Jonathan Wolff's moral philosophy, 42 chapters of George Herbert Mead's social psychology (`Mind, Self, and Society`), and 7 modules from Noam Chomsky, Michel Foucault, and Jacques Derrida.

When characters face betrayal, sacrifice, coercion, or intimate conflict in LLM roleplay, models tend to make two opposite mistakes: either characters act like frictionless moral calculators who never suffer psychological aftershocks, or they speak like omniscient modern psychotherapists who immediately diagnose their own childhood trauma in polished clinical terminology.

**Moral Ethics 2.1** solves both problems by uniting **macro-normative ethics** (*What is right? Who pays the price?*) with **micro-sociological interactionism and clinical Gestalt mechanics** (*How do gestures call out responses? How does the spontaneous `"I"` clash with the socially monitored `"Me"`? How do characters misread others through their own past pain?*).

## 2. General CoT, Logic Gate & Non-Single-Book Entries (`unidentifiable_entries.md`)

Cross-referenced with `unidentifiable_entries.md`, **8 entries in `Moral Ethics 2.1.json`** serve as system control gates, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) verifiers, general school-of-thought syntheses (`[Gestalt]`), or original micro-reasoning rules (`[SELF-REASONING]`):

### A. Master Logic Gate, Selector, CoT & Audit Ledger (`UID 0, 16, 17, 18` at `@D0`)
1. **`UID 0` — `[ETHICS:CORE] Moral Ethics Engine` (`Constant, @D0, Order 100`)**
   * **Dynamic `{MoralPressure}` Gatekeeping:**
     * **Low-Pressure Mode (`{MoralPressure} < 70` and no major casualties/betrayal):** Runs silently in the background and appends only a compact tag at the end of prose: `[Moral State: Stable | Accumulated Debt: {Debt}]`.
     * **High-Pressure / Crisis Mode (`{MoralPressure} >= 70` or Major Ethical Event):** Activates `ETHICS:COT` and `ETHICS:AUDIT`, enforces the **Law of Moral Entropy** (rejecting painless "everybody wins" compromises when resources and time are scarce), and prevents calculating utilitarian options in cold arithmetic without emotional cost.
2. **`UID 18` — `[ETHICS:SELECTOR] Engineering Enhancement: Ethical Module Selector and Consequence Ledger` (`Constant, @D0, Order 110`)**
   * Routes each scene to the minimum necessary combination of **Wolff** (`UID 1–15`, normative justification), **Mead** (`UID 19–60`, gestures, `"I"` vs `"Me"`, generalized other), **Chomsky/Foucault/Derrida** (`UID 61–67`, speech interfaces, self-writing, linguistic violence), and **Self-Reasoning** (`UID 71`, experience bias), while tracking a 3-layer ethical consequence ledger.
3. **`UID 16` — `[ETHICS:COT] Ethical Chain-of-Thought (COT) Verification` (`Keyword, @D0, Order 150`)**
   * Enforces a structured 4-step reasoning block inside `<think>` before prose generation:
     * `Step 1: Conflict Identification` (Moral vs. Moral or Moral vs. Self-Interest)
     * `Step 2: Socratic Self-Check` (Testing for double standards, Gyges' Ring temptation, or means-end rationalization)
     * `Step 3: Moral Calculus` (Weighing Utilitarian utility, Kantian universalizability, and Aristotelian virtue)
     * `Step 4: Mead Micro-Psychological Verification` (Mapping the significant symbols, the `"Me"`'s internalized community censorship, the `"I"`'s impulsive response, and how the `"I"` transforms into a scarred new `"Me"`).
4. **`UID 17` — `[ETHICS:AUDIT] Ethical Audit` (`Keyword, @D0, Order 160`)**
   * Generates the `<Moral_Audit>` Ledger-Style Balance Sheet tracking `[Active Framework]`, `[Primary Value]`, `[Action Taken]`, `[Moral Debt Delta]`, `[Alienation Index]`, and `[Mead Self-Split: I-Impulse vs Me-Monitoring]`.

### B. Gestalt Therapy Synthesis & Original Experience-Bias Engine (`UID 68–71` at `@D1`)
5. **`UID 68` — `[Gestalt] Character Need Perception and Behavioral Chain Reasoning Engine` (`Constant, @D1, Order 120`)**
   * Converts the Gestalt **Cycle of Experience** (*Sensation → Awareness → Mobilization of Energy → Action → Contact → Withdrawal*) into the baseline physiological-motivational chain for every character action.
6. **`UID 69` — `[Gestalt] Character Psychological Defense Mechanisms and Unhealthy Interpersonal Interaction Reasoning` (`Keyword, @D1, Order 181`)**
   * Models Gestalt **Boundary Disturbances** (*Introjection, Projection, Retroflection, Deflection, Confluence*) as survival-driven creative adjustments under relational stress.
7. **`UID 70` — `[Gestalt] Subconscious Trauma Intervention and Attention Focus Reasoning` (`Keyword, @D1, Order 182`)**
   * Uses Gestalt **Figure-Ground** formation and **Unfinished Business** (Zeigarnik effect) to deduce how unhealed obsessions hijack a character's attention in the present.
8. **`UID 71` — `[SELF-REASONING] Personal Experience Reasoning Bias and Trauma Misrecognition` (`Keyword, @D1, Order 180`)**
   * Original roleplay micro-reasoning module (`self_reasoning_experience_bias_added_20260805`): forces characters to interpret others' motives through their own past survival templates first, and models **delayed/somatic trauma recognition** (recognizing pain first as insomnia, nausea, defensive anger, or over-compliance rather than clinical labels).
