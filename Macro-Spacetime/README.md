# Macro Spacetime (`宏观时空`)

[ ← Back to Repository Index ](../../README.md) &nbsp;•&nbsp; [ 简体中文 ](README-zh.md)

**A 154-entry cross-worldview macro-sociological, political, legal, economic, and living-world reasoning engine for SillyTavern roleplay.**


## 1. Overview & Core Function

* **Source Files:** `Macro Spacetime.json` (English) / `宏观时空.json` (Chinese)
* **Total Entries:** **154 Entries** (`5 Constant` core control entries / `149 Keyword-Triggered` domain modules and resolvers)
* **Companion World Book Note:** While the master router (`[CORE:ALL]`) retains routing hooks for 26 academic frameworks so the entire ecosystem can operate together, the pre-modern/medieval church, royal-body, and military-order modules (`MEDTHEO:KANT`, `PROTCAP:WEBER`, `MAGICMICRO:GINZBURG`, `PURGATORY:LEGOFF`, `LOUDUN:HUXLEY`, `MONSTER:SMITH`, `RIDERCORPS:DAVENPORT`, `CYBERPOL:MEDINA`, `FOUCAULT:RIVIERE`, `TEUTONIC:*`) are housed in the companion [Middle Ages (中世纪)](https://www.google.com/search?q=../Middle-Ages/README.md) World Book.

When roleplaying in expansive settings—whether a galactic empire, a cyberpunk megacity, an alternate-history republic, or a high-fantasy continent—LLMs often struggle with **macro-to-micro translation**. High-level politics either degenerates into abstract textbook summaries or collapses into personal melodrama where kings, rebels, corporations, and academies ignore logistics, fiscal limits, bureaucratic distortion, and information latency.

**Macro Spacetime** solves this by pairing a **5-entry permanent routing core** with **149 keyword-triggered secondary reasoning entries** distilled from 18 foundational works in legal philosophy, political theology, postcolonial studies, peasant resistance, social movements, comparative revolutions, structural anthropology, sociology of organizations, scientific paradigms, structuration theory, field/habitus sociology, structural Marxism, biopolitics, and colonial literature.

## 2. General CoT, System Routers & Non-Book Entries (`unidentifiable_entries.md`)

Cross-referenced with `unidentifiable_entries.md`, `Macro Spacetime.json` contains **8 entries that do not belong to a single published book** (4 `[CORE:ALL]` system routers + 4 `[Living World]` / `[活的世界]` dynamic roleplay engines), alongside **3 Cross-Book Integrated Resolvers** that synthesize multiple books into actionable crisis checklists:

### A. Permanent System Control & Routing Engine (`[CORE:ALL]` — 4 Constant Entries)
1. **`UID 0` — `[CORE:ALL] Cross-Worldview Macro-Narrative Master Control Engine` (`Constant, @D0, Order 100`)**
   * Consolidates the macro-sociological frameworks into a unified background reasoning system and enforces the **Anti-Jargon Rule**: all theoretical mechanisms must manifest through concrete personnel, ledgers, decrees, terrain friction, prices, and dialogue subtext.
2. **`UID 1` — `[CORE:ALL] Worldview Terminology Translator and Trigger Router` (`Constant, @D0, Order 110`)**
   * Translates setting-native nouns into institutional functions before selecting a reasoning framework (e.g., *dragon pact / canon law / algorithmic protocol* → `HART`; *frontier / foreign race / survey expedition* → `ORIENT` + `SCOTT`; *spell paradigm / academy orthodoxy* → `KUHN:SSR`; *chartered mining guild / colonial outpost* → `CONRAD:HOD`).
3. **`UID 2` — `[CORE:ALL] Three-Layer Propagation and Consequence Ledger` (`Constant, @D1, Order 120`)**
   * Requires every macro event to propagate across three distinct layers:
     * **Direct Layer:** Immediate reactions of protagonists, enforcers, clerks, soldiers, and bystanders on the scene.
     * **Institutional Layer:** How courts, bureaucracies, guilds, garrisons, and archives record, distort, or weaponize the event.
     * **Everyday Layer:** Ripple effects on grain prices, curfews, tavern rumors, checkpoints, and household survival.
4. **`UID 74` — `[CORE:ALL] Near-Field Minimum Mechanism Selector` (`Constant, @D1, Order 130`)**
   * Prevents prompt overload by selecting at most **2–3 macro mechanisms** per turn and enforcing a strict **70% foreground sensory/character action vs. 30% structural pressure** ratio.

### B. Original Dynamic Roleplay Engine (`[Living World]` / `[活的世界]` — 1 Constant + 3 Keyword Entries)
5. **`UID 218` — `[Living World] Anti-Omniscience` (`[活的世界] 反全知`, `Constant, @D1, Order 140`)**
   * Enforces strict **Double-Blind Information Asymmetry**: NPCs cannot read the player's status sheet, unvoiced inner monologue, or hidden inventory; they react strictly based on rumors, forged papers, dress, class bias, and delayed dispatches.
6. **`UID 215` — `[Living World] Random Conflict Event Generation` (`[活的世界] 随机冲突事件生成`, `Keyword, @D2, Order 395`)**
   * Replaces arbitrary dice-roll encounters with the physical collision of spatial ecology (narrow alleys, checkpoints, black markets) and competing faction schedules.
7. **`UID 216` — `[Living World] Strong Relationality and Feuds & Gratitude` (`[活的世界] 强关系性与恩怨情仇`, `Keyword, @D2, Order 396`)**
   * Replaces numerical affection bars with a concrete **Narrative Debt Ledger** (life-saving grace, blood feuds, blackmail leverage, factional oaths).
8. **`UID 217` — `[Living World] Combat Scenes` (`[活的世界] 战斗场景`, `Keyword, @D2, Order 397`)**
   * Grounds micro-level violence in center-of-gravity control, momentum, physiological exhaustion, weapon reach, and environmental obstacles rather than abstract HP pools.

### C. Cross-Book Integrated Crisis Resolvers (`UID 71–73` — Listed in both `identifiable_entries.md` & `unidentifiable_entries.md`)
* **`UID 71` — `[CORE:HART+THEO+POLPHIL] Sovereignty, Legitimacy, and Crisis Resolver` (`Keyword, @D3, Order 371`):** 4-step adjudication matrix for martial law, coups, succession crises, and tax revolts *(Hart + Schmitt + Wolff)*.
* **`UID 72` — `[CORE:ORIENT+ORG+SCOTT] Imperial Governance, Center-Periphery, and Counter-Archive Resolver` (`Keyword, @D3, Order 372`):** 10-step adjudication matrix for frontier governance, chartered companies, local elite collusion, and highland tax evasion *(Said + Zhou Xueguang + Scott + Conrad)*.
* **`UID 73` — `[CORE:MYTH+THEO] Myth-Orthodoxy Structure Resolver` (`Keyword, @D3, Order 373`):** Adjudicates prophecies, sacred relics, doctrinal schisms, and divine kingship *(Lévi-Strauss + Schmitt)*.
