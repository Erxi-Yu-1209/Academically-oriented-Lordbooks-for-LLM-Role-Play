# Middle Ages (`中世纪`)

**An 88-entry, zero-permanent-overhead SillyTavern World Book for medieval, early-modern, crusade mystery, and dark-fantasy roleplay.**


## 1. Overview & Core Function

* **Source Files:** `Middle Ages.json` (English) / `中世纪.json` (Chinese)
* **Total Entries:** **88 Entries** (`0 Constant` / `88 Keyword-Triggered`)
* **Injection Architecture:** Distributed across **Depth `@D4`** (royal political theology, Reformation commercial spirit, Roman equestrian bureaucracy, state cybernetics, Teutonic statutory/supply structures), **Depth `@D3`** (the spatialization and pastoral economy of Purgatory, Teutonic patronage/castles/Levant politics), and **Depth `@D2`** (inquisitorial microhistory, Loudun possession theater, Gévaudan monster panics, the Rivière judicial dossier, and Teutonic front-line mystery reasoning).

In medieval, historical, or dark-fantasy roleplay, LLMs often flatten pre-modern institutions into anachronistic modern clichés: kings rule like absolute dictators without liturgical or corporate constraints, inquisitors act like cartoon villains without archival questionnaires, monsters are treated as simple RPG boss fights rather than socio-political crises, and military orders (like the Knights Templar, Hospitaller, or Teutonic Knights) are reduced to generic paladins in shining armor.

**Middle Ages** provides **88 keyword-triggered secondary reasoning modules** distilled from 10 landmark historical and sociological monographs. Because **all 88 entries have `constant: false`**, this World Book consumes **zero tokens** during ordinary scenes and wakes up only when the narrative touches its specific institutional triggers—whether alone or stacked alongside [Macro Spacetime](https://www.google.com/search?q=../Macro-Spacetime/README.md).

## 2. Module Master Controls & Entry-Point Detectors (Internal CoT Gates)

While all 88 entries in `Middle Ages.json` trace directly to published historical works in `identifiable_entries.md`, **9 entries serve as module-level Master Controls or Detectors** that instruct the LLM on *when* and *how* to initialize that specific historical reasoning framework:

1. **`UID 50` — `[MEDTHEO:KANT] Medieval King's Two Bodies Master Control: Body Natural and Body Politic` (`@D4, Order 450`):** Initializes Kantorowicz's distinction between the monarch's mortal *Body Natural* and the undying corporate *Body Politic* (Crown, realm, liturgy, and inalienable estate).
2. **`UID 57` — `[PROTCAP:WEBER] Reformation to Commercial Spirit Master Control: Protestant Fission Within the Old Order` (`@D4, Order 457`):** Initializes Weber's framework when urban crafts, double-entry bookkeeping, time discipline, and ascetic reformist sects emerge within an older liturgical-feudal order.
3. **`UID 75` — `[MAGICMICRO:GINZBURG] Recording Method for Fantasy Concepts and Microhistory Entry Point` (`@D2, Order 275`):** Instructs the LLM not to immediately declare folk cosmologies, visions, or heresies "true or false," but to trace how minor figures propose them in mills and taverns and how inquisitorial scribes reclassify them.
4. **`UID 84` — `[PURGATORY:LEGOFF] Third Place Detector: When a World Needs Purgatory` (`@D3, Order 344`):** Activates Le Goff's logic whenever a setting moves beyond a binary heaven/hell to govern the "not yet wholly pure" dead through measurable time, geographical entrances, and intercessory masses.
5. **`UID 92` — `[LOUDUN:HUXLEY] Political Theatre of Possession Detector` (`@D2, Order 292`):** Activates Huxley's Loudun logic when demonic possession entangles enclosed convents/temples, physicians, royal commissioners, urban spectators, and factional scapegoating.
6. **`UID 101` — `[MONSTER:SMITH] Birth of a Monster Detector: From Attacks to Monster` (`@D2, Order 301`):** Activates Jay M. Smith's Gévaudan logic to show how terrain, vulnerable pastoral labor, werewolf folklore, episcopal sermons, failed royal hunts, and the press jointly manufacture a "Beast."
7. **`UID 110` — `[RIDERCORPS:DAVENPORT] Equestrian Bureaucrat Detector: How Mounted Status Becomes a Governing Class` (`@D4, Order 510`):** Activates Davenport's Roman Equestrian framework for an auditable second-tier elite (property threshold, state horse, officer-to-procurator career ladder, and reserved theater seating).
8. **`UID 119` — `[CYBERPOL:MEDINA] Techno-Political Interaction Detector: Technology Is Not a Neutral Tool` (`@D4, Order 519`):** Activates Medina's sociotechnical governance logic whenever crystal dashboards, beacon networks, or statistical operations rooms are used to manage a realm.
9. **`UID 202` — `[TEUTONIC:CORE] Military Order Mystery Master Control: Triple Identity of Hospital, Knightly Order, and Frontier State` (`@D2, Order 530`):** Master control for the 20-entry Teutonic Knights module (`UID 202–221`), culminating in `UID 221 [TEUTONIC:MYSTERY_METHOD]` (solving medieval institutional mysteries through a fivefold cross-check of **vows, statutes, ledgers, seals, and logistics routes**).
